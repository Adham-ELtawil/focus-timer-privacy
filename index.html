Privacy Policy for Focus Timer

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Focus Timer ("the extension") is a productivity tool that provides a countdown timer, stopwatch, and Pomodoro-style focus timer. This Privacy Policy explains how the extension handles information.

Data Collection

Focus Timer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing activity, or usage analytics. The extension does not use any remote servers, analytics tools, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Local Storage

The extension uses the browser's local storage (via the storage permission) only to save your own preferences on your own device, such as:

  • Timer duration and Pomodoro settings
  • Quick preset values

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Permissions Used

  • alarms — to schedule and trigger timer and Pomodoro session alerts.
  • notifications — to notify you when a session, break, or timer ends.
  • storage — to save your preferences locally on your device.
  • offscreen — to play a notification sound when a timer completes.

Third-Party Sharing

Focus Timer does not sell, transfer, or share any user data with third parties, because it does not collect any user data in the first place.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach the developer, Adham Eltawil, via the contact information on the Chrome Web Store listing.
